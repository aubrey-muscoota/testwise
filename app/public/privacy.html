Privacy Policy

Testwise · Last updated November 6, 2026

Testwise is a practice app for standardized tests, made by Muscoota. This page explains what Testwise stores, why, who else sees it, and how to delete it.

Who can use Testwise

Testwise is for people 13 and older. We ask for your birth month and year before you start. We use the answer only to check your age and do not store your birthday. If you are under 13, Testwise does not create an account or keep anything about you.

What we store

Testwise does not ask for your name, email or phone number. Your device keeps a random learner ID, and we store the following with it:

  • Your answers to practice questions, how long each one took, your progress per skill, your streak and XP, and which questions are due for review.
  • Your chats with Socrates, the AI tutor, if you choose to use it.
  • The date you confirmed your age and, if you agree, the date you turned on Socrates.
  • Your time zone offset, so streaks follow your local day.

If you report a question, we keep your note and the first six characters of your learner ID, so we can fix the question.

We use this information only to run Testwise: to pick your next questions, track progress, and improve our questions and tutor. We don't sell it, don't show ads and don't use it for advertising.

Socrates and Anthropic

Socrates is powered by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Socrates only turns on after you agree in the app. When you chat with Socrates, we send Anthropic the question, the correct answer and solution, your answer, and the messages you type, so it can write replies. Anthropic processes this data to provide the service to us and does not use it to train its models. Please don't type personal details in the chat. You can turn Socrates off at any time from the Home screen.

How long we keep it

We keep your practice data while you use Testwise. You can delete it at any time with "Delete my data" at the bottom of the Home screen. That permanently removes your progress, streak and Socrates chats from our database.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
