Delete your data

You can delete everything Testwise stores about you from the app, at any time. It takes effect right away and can't be undone.

From the app

  1. Open Testwise on the device you practise on.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. Tap "Download my data" first if you'd like a copy. It saves one file with everything we hold.
  4. Tap "Delete my data" and confirm.

That removes your progress, streak, answers and Socrates chats from our database. The app then starts fresh on that device.

If you no longer have the device

Testwise doesn't store your name or email with your practice data, only a random ID kept on the device. Without the device we can't tell which data is yours, so it can't be found or linked back to you.

Your beta sign-up

If you joined the beta, your email is kept separately from your practice data. To remove it, write to us: aubrey@muscoota.com
