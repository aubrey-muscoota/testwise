From your first question to test day

  1. Find your level

    A 12-question placement quiz marks the skills you already know as done, so you start where you are.

  2. Get a plan to your test date

    Tell us when you test and how many minutes you have each day. The plan works back from the date, with a rest day each week.

  3. Do one short lesson a day

    Eight questions at the level you get about two in three right. A question you miss comes back tomorrow, then a few days later, until it sticks.

  4. Keep your streak

    Every day with a finished lesson adds to your streak. Miss a day and the plan moves the work forward instead of piling it up.

Socrates asks before he tells

Most apps show the solution when you get one wrong. Socrates looks at the answer you picked, works out the likely slip, and asks you one question at a time until you see it yourself.

He works from each question's checked answer and worked solution, so he never argues with the key. He only turns on after you say yes.
