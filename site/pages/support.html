Support

Short answers to what students ask most. If yours isn't here, write to us: aubrey@muscoota.com

Can I use Testwise on another phone or computer?

Not yet. Your progress is tied to the device you started on, because Testwise doesn't ask for an account. Sign-in, so your progress follows you, is coming next.

How does the streak work?

Finish at least one lesson in a day and your streak goes up by one. Days follow your own time zone. If today isn't done yet, yesterday's streak is still alive until midnight.

A question looks wrong

Tap "Report it" under the question after you answer. We read every report and fix or remove the question.

Socrates isn't answering

Socrates turns on only after you agree, from a lesson or the Me tab. You get five chats a day, up to six messages each. When you've used them, the written hints for each question are still there.

I'm under 13

Testwise is for students 13 and older, so the app won't create a profile for you. That's a rule we keep to protect younger students' privacy.
